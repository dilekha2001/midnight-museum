# Additional Van Gogh collection

Five public-domain paintings by Vincent van Gogh, stored locally in public/art. Metadata was checked against the Art Institute of Chicago open collection API. Reproduction images were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons; the museum image server was unavailable.

- **The Bedroom (1889)** — [Museum record](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/28560), [image source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vincent_van_Gogh_-_The_Bedroom_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg). Local file: vangogh-bedroom.jpg.
- **Self-Portrait (1887)** — [Museum record](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/80607), [image source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vincent_van_Gogh_-_Self-Portrait_-_1954.326_-_Art_Institute_of_Chicago.jpg). Local file: vangogh-self-portrait.jpg.
- **The Poet’s Garden (1888)** — [Museum record](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/14586), [image source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Poet's_Garden_1888_Vincent_van_Gogh.jpg). Local file: vangogh-poets-garden.jpg.
- **The Drinkers (1890)** — [Museum record](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/79349), [image source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vincent_van_Gogh_-_The_Drinkers_-_1953.178_-_Art_Institute_of_Chicago.jpg). Local file: vangogh-drinkers.jpg.
- **Fishing in Spring (1887)** — [Museum record](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/109314), [image source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vincent_van_Gogh_-_Fishing_in_Spring%2C_the_Pont_de_Clichy_(Asni%C3%A8res)_-_1965.1169_-_Art_Institute_of_Chicago.jpg). Local file: vangogh-fishing-spring.jpg.
