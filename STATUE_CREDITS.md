# Heritage sculpture scans — credits and reuse

The following real museum-object scans are by **Scan the World**, distributed through 3DBigDataSpace on Zenodo. They depict objects at the British Museum; the project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the museum.

All three source records specify **Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–ShareAlike 4.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/. These model assets are for noncommercial use under those terms. Keep attribution and license notices with redistributed copies. This asset license is separate from the game’s code and the public-domain painting images. Commercial distribution would require separately licensed replacement models or appropriate permission.

- **Head of a Marble Kouros**. Source title: Marble Kouros at The British Museum, London. https://zenodo.org/records/21162491. Local asset: `public/models/kouros.glb`. Source file: `a074fa4a9e00e8a33c0d6b2cb478d889_normalized_optimized-0.500.glb`. Approx. 50,000 triangles; 589 KB. Displayed upright at pedestal scale. The artifact is a surviving head, not a reconstructed full figure. The c. 600–480 BCE context refers to the Archaic kouros tradition rather than a verified individual object date.
- **Woman Wearing a Peplos**. https://zenodo.org/records/21248270. Local asset: `public/models/peplos.glb`. Source file: `f50186f7e980f26828a103d780d26732_normalized_optimized-0.500.glb`. Approx. 50,000 triangles; 692 KB. Source describes early Classical sculpture and the figures associated with Building G at Xanthos. The identity of the figure is uncertain.
- **Headless Seated Buddha**. https://zenodo.org/records/21164784. Local asset: `public/models/seated-buddha.glb`. Source file: `8bb8bc0d3df867a2e7540ae2c0e65993_normalized_optimized.glb`. Approx. 100,000 triangles; 2.21 MB. Displayed as the surviving fragment. The source record does not provide an exact date or origin, so the game does not invent them.

Original GLB files are distributed unchanged. Runtime changes are placement, orientation, uniform scaling, and nonmetallic stone roughness for the museum lighting. Source records and reference preview images are included under `public/models/credits/`. The file checksums are verified by the model tests.

The locally bundled Draco decoder comes from the installed Three.js package. Three.js is MIT-licensed (`public/draco/THREE-LICENSE.txt`); Draco is Apache-2.0-licensed (see `public/draco/DRACO-LICENSE.txt`). No decoder CDN or model account is required to play.
